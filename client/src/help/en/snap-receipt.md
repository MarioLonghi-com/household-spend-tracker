Snap is a small page made for a phone. Photograph the receipt at the till and it goes straight to the receipts inbox, ready to be matched when the statement arrives.

## Take a photo

1. On your phone, open the app and choose **Snap a Receipt** in the menu. If you are signed out, you sign in first and come back to it.
2. Check the household at the top right. If you belong to more than one, tap it to switch. The page wears that household's colour, so a receipt does not land in the wrong one.
3. Tap the big camera button, *Take a photo*, and photograph the receipt. For photos you already took, use *Add several from the library*, where you can choose many at once.
4. Each picture is compressed on the phone and sent. Once it shows as in the inbox, you can type what it was for in the box under it. That is saved as the receipt notes.

Your browser can add the page to the home screen, so it opens like an app. *Go to app* takes you back to the full app.

## Nothing gets lost

Photos stay on the phone until the server has them. With no signal, take the photo anyway: it waits in the queue and is sent the next time you open Snap with a connection. A photo that could not be sent shows *Retry*.

## Location

The location button is off until you switch it on. While it is off, a photo records only what the camera wrote into it, which may already include where it was taken.

Switched on, every picture you send also records where the phone is at that moment, used only when the picture has no location of its own. It goes to this household's server and nowhere else. Because it is where the phone is now, a photo from your library taken somewhere else gets the place you send it from. The choice is remembered on that phone.

Browsers offer location only over a secure (https) connection, so on a plain one the button stays unavailable and the page says why.

## Then

Receipts from Snap wait on the [Receipts](screen:receipts) screen. To put one on its transaction, see [Attach a receipt to a transaction](help:attach-receipt).
