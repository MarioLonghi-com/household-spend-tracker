Categories say what a transaction was for, and they sit in groups. They are classification only: there are no budgets or amounts on the [Categories](screen:categories) screen.

## Add a group or a category

1. Open **Categories** from your household's menu.
2. For a new group, press **Add a group**, type a **Name** and press **Create**.
3. For a new category, press **Add a category** on the card of the group it belongs in, type a **Name** and press **Create**.

A household with no categories yet offers **Start with a common set** or **Build my own** instead. Two categories cannot share a name, even in different groups, and neither can two groups.

## Rename or move

1. Press **Edit** on the category's row.
2. Change its **Name**, or choose another **Group** to move it there.
3. Press **Save**.

To rename a group, press the group's name at the top of its card, change the **Name** and press **Save**.

## Archive or delete

The **Transactions** column says how many transactions carry each category, and that decides what you can do with it.

- *Archive* a category you have used but no longer want. Press **Edit**, tick **Archived — hide it from the pickers** and press **Save**. It leaves the pickers and stays on every transaction it already categorised, so the past still reads correctly. Tick **Show archived** at the top to see archived categories again; untick the box in the panel to bring one back.
- *Delete* is offered only when the column says **unused**. Press **Edit**, then **Delete**, and confirm; **Keep it** backs out. A payee that was set to always use that category loses that setting.
- A group can be deleted only when no category is under it, archived ones included. Press the group's name, then **Delete group**. If it refuses although the card looks empty, tick **Show archived**: the group still holds archived categories. Move them to another group first.

Every delete is one entry in [History](screen:history), and **Undo this** there brings it back.

## The Income group and the Reimbursement category

The [Income vs Expense](screen:report-income-expense) report counts the categories of one group as income and everything else as spending. That group wears the **Income** tag. To give the role to another group, press its name, tick **The Income group — its categories are income on Income vs Expense** and press **Save**; the old group loses it.

In the same way, one category can wear the **Reimbursement** tag, for money work pays back. Tick **The Reimbursement category — money work pays back** in its panel to move the role there. The report then shows those rows apart, as [work reimbursements](help:reimbursements).

## Who is behind a category

The **Payees** column counts the different payees in each category, and a category's panel lists the busiest of them. Sort by any column heading; sorting by **Transactions** brings the unused ones together. To choose which category a payee's new transactions get, see [Name and categorise payees automatically](help:payee-rules).
