When you pay for something your employer will pay back, such as a hotel or a taxi to a client, mark it as a work expense. The app then keeps track of what work still owes you, links the repayment when it arrives, and keeps both out of your own income and spending.

## Mark a work expense

1. In [Transactions](screen:register), click the payment to open its panel.
2. Set **Reimbursement** to **Work should pay this back**.

A *W* appears at the end of the memo, and the panel shows how many days it has been waiting. Only money going out can be a work expense, and never a transfer. It also works on a locked transaction.

For several at once, tick them in the register and choose **Work should pay these back** in the **Work expense…** list of the bar that appears. Rows that cannot be work expenses are left alone, and a note says how many.

## Link the repayment

When the money from work arrives:

1. Open the work expense and choose **Find the payment…**.
2. The list shows money coming in within 45 days either side of the expense (either side, because an advance can arrive first). Choose the payment and then **Link**.

Or tick the expenses and the one payment together in the register and choose **Link as reimbursement**. One payment can repay many expenses. Open the payment to see what it repays, how much that covers and any difference.

To link a different payment, use **Change** in the expense's panel. To take the link off, use **Not reimbursed after all**.

Give the payment from work the category marked as the Reimbursement category on the [Categories](screen:categories) screen. A new household has one, *Reimbursements*.

## When work will not pay

Set **Reimbursement** to **Written off — work will not pay it**. The expense then counts as your own spending. If work paid only part of it, [split](help:split-transaction) the expense, link the part that was paid and write off the rest.

## The Reimbursements report

[Reimbursements](screen:report-reimbursements), under **Reports**, shows **Work owes you**, **Recovered**, **Written off**, and money received that no expense accounts for yet. Below are the **Outstanding** expenses, the **Payments from work** with what each one covered, and a view **By month**. Each currency keeps its own figures: nothing is converted or added across currencies.

**Show in Transactions** opens the register on what is still owed. The **Work expenses** filter in the register offers the same views at any time.

## In Income vs Expense

Work expenses that are waiting or repaid, and the repayments in the Reimbursement category, are shown apart under **Work reimbursements**, not as your income or spending. Only the difference counts: if work paid more than the linked expenses, the extra is income; if it paid less, the shortfall is a cost. A written-off expense is ordinary spending.
