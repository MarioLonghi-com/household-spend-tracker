The first time the app starts, a setup wizard creates you, your household and its starting categories. Afterwards, the household's own page is where its settings live.

## The first-run wizard

The steps sit on a rail at the side (a progress bar on a phone). **Back**, or a step on the rail, returns to any step you have reached. Nothing is saved until the last step.

1. Choose your **Language**. It re-words the wizard at once and names the starter categories.
2. Read the overview of how the app fits together. It changes nothing.
3. On a server install, paste the **Setup token**: the step shows the command that prints it. Then give your name, email and a password of at least 12 characters.
4. On a server install, add a second step: **Use a passkey** or **Use an authenticator app**. On a Mac with Touch ID you can choose **Open with Touch ID** instead.
5. Keep your recovery code: **Copy** or **Save as file**, then tick **I have stored this somewhere other than this computer**.
6. Say who the household is for (this only suggests a name), then set the **Household name** and its **Colour**.
7. Pick a **Country**. It suggests the **Base currency**, the **Date format** and the **First day of the week**, and a preview shows the result.
8. Choose starting categories: **Recommended**, **Essentials**, **Start empty**, or **Bring from YNAB** where it is offered. Untick anything you will never use; *Income* and *Reimbursements* stay, marked **needed by reports**.
9. Optionally add a first account with an **Opening date** (years back if you like) and an **Opening balance**, or **Skip for now**. A CSV or OFX file dropped here opens in Import once setup is done.
10. Check the summary, use **Change** to fix an answer, and press **Create my household**.

**Create my household** makes everything together or nothing at all. The categories are then your own words: changing the app language later does not rename them. The finish screen lists where to go next, and **Technical details** says where this installation keeps its data and how it is backed up.

## Change the household later

Press your household's name in the menu to open its page. Under **Settings** you can change:

- the **Name**;
- the **Main currency**, used only for totals across accounts; each account keeps its own, and nothing is converted;
- the **First day of the week**, where weekly totals start;
- a **Note**;
- the **Colour**: a palette, and **Your own accent (optional)**; **Use the palette's** drops the accent. The colour tells two households apart at a glance;
- **Receipts**: *Readable copies only*, or **Also keep the original file**, which takes about 70 times the space.

Press **Save**. Anyone in the household can change these. The date format chosen during setup has no field on this page.

## People and more households

**What is in it** counts the household's accounts, transactions, payees and more, and **Who is in it** lists its people. Everyone in a household can do everything in it; only an owner changes who is in it, from [Admin](screen:admin). On a server install, **Invitations** there has **Invite somebody**, which makes a one-time link. A further household is made under **Households** with **New household**.

To bring in a whole history from YNAB, see [One-time import](help:one-time-import). To organise the categories, see [Organise categories and groups](help:categories).
