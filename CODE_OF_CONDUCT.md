# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). What
follows is a short adaptation of it; where the two differ, the Covenant's text
is the one that applies.

## The pledge

Everyone who takes part here -- in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions
and anywhere else the project is represented -- does so without harassment,
whatever the other person's age, body, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race,
caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good faith.
- Disagree about the work, not about the person, and accept that the answer
  may still be no.
- Give and take criticism about code as information, not as a verdict.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to the people they affected, and learn from them.
- Keep other people's data out of the tracker's issues. This is a finance
  project: a real statement, name or account number posted here is somebody's
  privacy, including your own.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information -- an address, an email, a
  bank detail -- without their explicit permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, and wherever someone is representing the
project in public: using an official address, posting from an official
account, or acting as its representative at an event.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report anything you see or experience to **info@mariolonghi.com**. Reports are
read by the maintainer and kept confidential. This is a one-maintainer project,
so the maintainer is the one who decides; if a report concerns the maintainer,
say so in it, and it will be handled with that conflict stated openly.

The response follows the Covenant's enforcement ladder, in proportion to what
happened: a private **correction** with an explanation; a **warning** with
consequences for continuing; a **temporary ban** from interaction with the
project; and, for a sustained pattern or a single serious act, a **permanent
ban**. Whoever reported it is told what was done.
